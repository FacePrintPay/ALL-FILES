## 4. The MyBuyo™ Biometric Gateway Platform

MyBuyo™ is presented as a retail biometric payment gateway specifically designed for self-checkout and integrated POS environments. Its core features include:

*   **Face Recognition Pay:** Customers enroll once and pay by looking at the terminal, offering a frictionless experience without cards or phones. Authentication uses encrypted biometric templates, not raw data.
*   **Fingerprint + Palm:** Provides multi-modal options for customer choice, with both fingerprint and palm authentication linked to a secure vault. This accommodates users who prefer not to use facial recognition.
*   **Self-Checkout Integration:** Designed as a drop-in SDK and API layer compatible with existing self-checkout terminals, requiring minimal retrofit.
*   **TotalRecall™ Vault:** Features SHA256-anchored forensic logging for every transaction, ensuring an immutable audit trail and compliance with Ahold Delhaize security standards.
*   **Privacy-First Architecture:** Emphasizes zero raw biometric storage, encrypted mathematical representations, and consent-based enrollment, aligning with BIPA, state privacy laws, and PCI DSS.
*   **Merchant Analytics Dashboard:** Offers real-time data on throughput, transaction velocity, enrolled customers, and shrink reduction, providing visibility into pilot performance.

## 6. Pilot Structure: The 3-Phase Rollout Plan

A structured, low-risk, three-phase rollout is proposed:

*   **Phase 01: Single-Store Pilot — Proof of Concept (Months 1–3):** Deployment on 2–4 self-checkout kiosks at one Food Lion location in North Carolina. Focus on enrollment rate, transaction velocity, customer satisfaction (NPS), and error rate.
*   **Phase 02: Regional Expansion — 5–10 Stores (Months 4–9):** Expansion to 5–10 NC/SC locations, integrating MyBuyo™ with the Food Lion MVP loyalty program. KPIs include checkout time reduction, repeat biometric usage, and shrink delta.
*   **Phase 03: Chain-Wide Rollout + Acquisition / Licensing Discussion (Months 10–18):** Full deployment across Food Lion's 1,100+ stores, presenting performance data to Ahold Delhaize USA executive team, and initiating discussions for licensing, acquisition, or exclusive partnership.

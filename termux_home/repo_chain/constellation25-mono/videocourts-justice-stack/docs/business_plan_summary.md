# VideoCourts.com – Business Plan Summary

## Market Opportunity

- **Target Market:** US courts, corrections facilities, attorneys, and the general public
- **Market Size:** $12.7B online legal services (2025) → $50B+ (2030)
- **Annual Court Filings:** 100M+ appearances nationwide
- **Cost Savings:** $50-300 per inmate transport × millions of appearances

## Core Offerings

### 1. Secure Virtual Hearings
- Multi-party video conferencing (Judge, Attorney, Defendant, Public, Reporter)
- Role-based permissions and breakout rooms
- High-quality audio/video with recording

### 2. Biometric Identity Verification
- ID.me integration for government-grade identity proofing
- Facial recognition login
- Multi-factor authentication
- Fraud prevention and audit trail

### 3. Evidence Preservation via TotalRecall™
- SHA-256 cryptographic hashing
- Chain of custody documentation
- CM/ECF-ready export bundles
- Immutable audit logs

### 4. AI-Augmented Workflows
- **Bailiff Agents™:** Real-time monitoring, security alerts
- **Clerk Assistant:** Docket prep, notifications, scheduling
- **Transcript AI:** Automated summarization and searchable transcripts

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Pilot Programs (Months 1-6)
- Target: 5 pilot courts in progressive jurisdictions
- Offer: Free 6-month pilot with white-glove onboarding
- Goal: Testimonials, case studies, cost savings documentation

### Phase 2: Regional Expansion (Months 7-18)
- Target: 50 courts across Southeast states
- Channels: Court technology conferences, state RFPs, direct outreach
- Partnerships: State bar associations, legal aid organizations

### Phase 3: National Scale (Months 19-36)
- Target: 500+ courts nationwide
- Approach: State government master service agreements
- Federal court integration (PACER)

### Phase 4: Acquisition (Years 3-5)
- Primary targets: Tyler Technologies, LexisNexis, Thomson Reuters
- Valuation: $150M-300M (3-6x revenue multiple)
- Exit strategy: Strategic acquisition or IPO

## Competitive Advantages

1. **Only AI-powered platform** with direct Tyler Technologies integration path

3. **Evidence integrity** via TotalRecall™ - forensic-grade chain of custody
4. **Multi-sided platform** - serves courts, attorneys, AND public
